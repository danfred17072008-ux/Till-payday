Till Payday Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Till Payday is a personal budgeting tool designed to help users track their available balance, upcoming bills, days until payday, safe daily spending and planned purchases.

Information entered into the app

Financial figures entered into Till Payday are used only to perform budgeting calculations within the app. Till Payday does not connect to bank accounts, provide loans, process payments or offer regulated financial advice.

Data collection and sharing

The current version of Till Payday does not require an account and does not collect, sell or share personal or financial information with the developer or other companies.

Data storage

Information entered into the app is stored locally on the user’s device. Users can remove this information by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Children

Till Payday is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page.
